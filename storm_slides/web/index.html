Inside this experiment

This is an educational 2D simulation of a synthetic specimen. Every reconstruction point comes from a fit to a simulated camera frame.

Camera & optics
64 × 64 pixels at 100 nm per pixel. A pixel-integrated Gaussian PSF has a fixed 120 nm standard deviation. Photon counts follow a Poisson model, with no read noise.
Blinking
A two-state process with adjustable off-to-on probability and a 40% on-to-off probability each frame. Photon yield varies across active frames. Photobleaching is not modeled.
Localization
Local maxima are fitted with a single-emitter Gaussian Poisson maximum-likelihood estimator. Overlapping emitters can bias fits. Reported precision is a Fisher-information estimate under that model; it is not a measured image resolution.
Drift correction
The switch subtracts the exact simulated drift. This demonstrates ideal correction; drift is not estimated from the images.
Images & export
Camera frames use one display scale across the acquisition. Widefield shows the mean of all frames, including future frames during playback. The reconstruction uses fixed-size display dots, not uncertainty kernels. CSV exports all accepted fits, both raw and corrected coordinates, precision, and acquisition settings. Frames in the interface are display previews.

Methods: Mortensen et al., 2010 · Thompson et al., 2002
